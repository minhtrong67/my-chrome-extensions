How it works

Three steps. That's it.

Add your images

Drag & drop, browse, paste with Ctrl+V, load a URL — or just right-click an image on any page.

Choose a format

Pick PNG, JPEG or WEBP, adjust quality, and optionally resize a single image.

Download

One image downloads directly. Several images arrive together in a single .zip.

Privacy first

Your images never leave your device

All decoding and encoding happens locally in your browser with the Canvas API. No servers, no accounts, no tracking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which formats are supported?

You can open PNG, JPEG, WEBP, GIF, BMP and SVG images and convert them to PNG, JPEG or WEBP.

Does it keep transparent backgrounds?

Yes for PNG and WEBP. JPEG has no transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white instead of black.

Is it really free and private?

Completely. Everything runs on your computer — nothing is uploaded and no data is collected.

Can I convert many images at once?

Yes. Add several files, choose a format and quality, and download them together as a single .zip.

Which browsers work?

Chrome 116 or newer and other Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, Opera…) that support Manifest V3.

Ready to convert?

Open the extension popup from your toolbar and drop in your first image.
