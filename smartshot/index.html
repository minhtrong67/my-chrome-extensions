Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Three capture modes, a fast annotation editor and a polished Material Design 3 interface.

Visible area

One click grabs exactly what's on screen — then export as PNG or JPEG.

Full page

Scrolls automatically and stitches a seamless tall image. Sticky headers appear only once.

Selected area

Drag a rectangle with a live size label to capture just the part you need.

Powerful editor

Pen, arrows, shapes, text and highlighter. Select, move, resize and recolor any annotation.

Irreversible redaction

Pixelate passwords and personal data. The exported pixels are truly gone.

Make it yours

Light, dark or system theme with 19 Material colors — and it follows your device language.

Auto language

Detects your device language and switches to English or Tiếng Việt automatically.

Keyboard friendly

Tool hotkeys, Ctrl+Z / Y, Ctrl+S to save and Ctrl+C to copy. Paste or drop any image to edit.

Private by design

Works fully offline with minimal permissions. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Load SmartShot as an unpacked extension in Chrome, Edge, Brave or any Chromium browser.

Download & unzip

Download the package and extract it to a permanent folder.

Open extensions

Go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.

Load unpacked

Click “Load unpacked” and choose the extracted SmartShot folder.

Pin & capture

Pin the blue icon to your toolbar and start capturing.
