# Privacy Policy — Video Downloader

Video Downloader does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There is no analytics and no remote server.

**What stays on your device**
- Your settings (theme, language, folder, …) are kept in the browser's local extension storage.
- The list of video links found in a tab is kept in session storage and deleted when the tab navigates or closes.

**Network requests**
- The extension only requests the video/stream URLs that you choose to download, from the site that serves them.
- If you select a Google font (Google Sans Flex, Roboto Flex, Roboto, Inter, Be Vietnam Pro) the settings window loads it from Google Fonts.

**Permissions** are listed in README.md and are used only for the features described there.
