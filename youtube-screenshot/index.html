Private by design

TubeShot asks for four things, and only uses them for capturing frames.

storageSaves your settings and capture history on your device.
downloadsSaves captured frames to your Downloads folder.
scriptingLoads TubeShot into a YouTube tab that was already open before you installed it.
youtube.com, m.youtube.comLets TubeShot read the video frame. It cannot run on any other website.
Nothing is sent to any server. No analytics, no accounts, no ads.

Install in a minute

TubeShot loads as an unpacked extension in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

  1. Download the project and unzip it.
  2. Open the extensions page:
    chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder that contains manifest.json.
  5. Pin TubeShot, open a YouTube video and click the icon.

Questions, answered

Do captures include the player controls?

No. TubeShot reads the video element directly, so the image contains only the video picture. A timestamp badge or caption appears only if you turn it on.

Why is my capture black?

Videos protected by DRM, such as some rentals and movies, cannot be read by any extension. Regular videos work normally.

How do I get the highest quality?

Set the YouTube player to the highest resolution first, then capture as PNG at 100 percent. The frame is saved at the video's native size.

Does it work on Shorts and live streams?

Yes. Shorts and live streams are supported. On live streams the progress bar is disabled because there is no fixed length.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Settings and history stay in your browser's local storage, and nothing is sent to a server.

Can I use it in Vietnamese?

Yes. TubeShot picks English or Tiếng Việt from your browser language, and you can change it in Settings at any time.
